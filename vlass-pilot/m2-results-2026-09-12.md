# M2 result: STOP, not discovery-search readiness

The frozen same-data experiment completed on 2026-09-12 with **STOP_M2**.
The exact replay reproduced the complete scientific JSON, including the two
failed gates. No thresholds, positions, exclusions, source code or input data
were changed after seeing these outputs. No new data were downloaded and no
unknown-source search was performed.

## Known-source and noise diagnostics

These are fixed-template amplitudes, not independently calibrated total-source
fluxes or validated historical light-curve points. Native measurements below
are the unchanged earlier full-plane results; common measurements use the M2
3.5-arcsec beam and common-image background MAD noise proxy.

| Epoch | Native amplitude (mJy) | Common amplitude (mJy) | Common noise proxy (mJy) | Common amplitude/noise | Peak offset (arcsec) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| QL2.1 | 0.966462 | 0.801144 | 0.189634 | 4.22469 | 0.32104 |
| QL3.1 | 1.001680 | 1.243339 | 0.187910 | 6.61668 | 0.32452 |
| QL4.1 | 1.005657 | 1.144534 | 0.183296 | 6.24419 | 0.29976 |

Localization passes in all epochs, but QL2.1 fails the frozen amplitude/noise
criterion. The known source's native/common changes cannot be treated as
variability: these are different measurements of the same images. The noiseless
point-source proof excludes a gross point-source unit-conversion failure under
the tested geometry; it does not establish that the real source, its background,
dirty-beam residuals or correlated image noise are described by that ideal model.
The present result does not distinguish these possible contributions.

The empirical grid nulls are **reference-epoch-screened, with no additional
cross-epoch masking**. They are not an unconditional random-sky sample.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| QL2.1 | 96 | -0.30896 | 1.07540 | 3.96043 | 0 |
| QL3.1 | 96 | 0.37046 | 1.65174 | 3.83589 | 0 |
| QL4.1 | 96 | 0.19786 | 1.41445 | 4.37497 | 0 |

The QL3.1 scatter exceeds the pre-set gate even though no held-out response
reaches 5. Development normalized MADs are 1.59531, 1.70765 and 1.75428,
respectively. This broader response distribution means the annular image MAD is
not yet a sufficiently validated uncertainty scale for the fitted amplitude.
Correlated noise, residual sky structure and spatial variation can contribute;
these outputs do not identify one as the sole cause. Zero observed tails in this
small conditional sample is not a survey-wide false-alarm calibration. Full
spatial-block summaries, including differing block medians/scatters, are retained
in the JSON; paired injections and repeated epochs do not multiply independent
sample size.

Common-reference median scale ratios relative to QL3.1 are 0.932050, 1 and
0.963945. Their stable halves and leave-one-out tests support this field's
bright-reference calibration feasibility only, not the nonvariability of every
reference or an uncertainty law for faint candidates.

## Complete accounting and selection limits

All 200 planned slots remain in the output. Exactly two are prospectively
excluded: held-out slot 10 has an original QL3.1 peak >=5 within its screening
radius, and development slot 184 is within the protected-position exclusion.
Neither was replaced. There are 102 usable development and 96 usable held-out
positions. No injected-stage selection errors or cross-epoch grid measurement
errors occurred.

Lower-flux recoveries had no promotion minimum, but their poor recovery is
important operational evidence. The passing bright-stratum medians do not
justify assuming equivalent performance near 1 mJy. Development 3 mJy QL4.1
median bias is +8.660%; it is retained rather than hidden by the held-out pass.

The original reference selector admits no 0.3, 0.5 or 1 mJy injections even
locally. All 40 usable 3 mJy injections are locally eligible but none enters the
original global top 12. All 39 usable 10 mJy injections enter the global top 12
and pass the final all-epoch common morphology gate (21 development, 18 held-out).
Consequently global-selection-conditioned bias is **unmeasured**, not zero, for
every stratum below 10 mJy. At 10 mJy the final held-out conditioned median biases
are the same values shown above. Selected-position measurements are explicitly
retained, with the synthetic source fixed at its true coordinates.

This sparse stratum design does not measure behavior near the global ranking
boundary. The real-data injection centres are integer QL3.1 pixels; only the
separate noiseless numerical proof spans the prescribed subpixel phases. These
limits prevent a general reference-selection-bias or blind-search completeness
claim.

## Handoff decision

Stop this frozen experiment here. Do not relax either failed gate, remove an
unfavorable null, promote the 10 mJy stratum to evidence for faint candidates,
or scan unknown targets. Any further VLASS experiment needs a separately frozen
question: first validate the fitted-amplitude noise model and real-source
native/common measurement differences, with confirmation data or controls not
treated as newly blind after inspection. That follow-up is a proposal, not an
executed continuation or a claim that the current data can establish a discovery.

## Executed evidence

The [original protocol](PROTOCOL.md) stopped at metadata-only QL1.1 version
ambiguity, preserved in [INITIAL-RESULT.md](INITIAL-RESULT.md) and acquisition.json.
The [separate follow-up](FOLLOWUP-PROTOCOL.md) prospectively used QL2.1/3.1/4.1.
Six science/RMS cutouts were anonymously retrieved from CADC SODA and hash-bound.
One final RMS request timed out at 75 seconds; a specifically authorized identical
single retry succeeded. Both the [stopped attempt](FOLLOWUP-ACQUISITION-STOP.md)
and acquisition-followup.json are preserved; acquisition-recovered.json adds the
new receipt without erasing the original failure.
Parent review exposed a Windows process-tree cleanup limitation in that timeout
wrapper. A later exact process inventory found no outstanding matching workers
and no late original RMS file, but termination of all descendants at the deadline
was not proved. [Transport audit and required hardening](TRANSPORT-AUDIT.md).

Stored follow-up metadata and FITS: **50,226,288 bytes**. Initial metadata adds
9,295 bytes; combined **50,235,583 bytes**, below the 250,000,000-byte cap. No
unknown field, raw visibility data or whole-sky catalogue was retrieved.

All three image/RMS pairs pass the specified field/campaign/DATE-OBS identity,
Jy/beam units, beam and WCS agreement checks. Each image is 1441 x 1441 with
100% finite science and positive finite RMS pixels. The same published sky
position was used without optimizing the target centroid.

# Delegated prospective label/power variant

Adopted September 6 under the user's instruction: "ok pursue now, and make the
decisions yourself". This resolves local research-design choices; it does not
authorize publication. It is an **exploratory declared variant**, not the original
August 23 registration. No DR4 data informed it. M8 synthetic outcomes were known.

The original commands, primary outputs, literal/difference sensitivities, original
GAP codes, EB26 byte-identical regression, fixed samples/configs, scope-pure primary,
pooled de-duplication, Holm families 3/5/6/1 and KEEP/REMOVE/CARRY remain unchanged.
Run the original pipeline first, then the separate read-only postprocessor.

- GAP-1: significant, expected direction without design power becomes
  `SIGNIFICANT, EXPECTED DIRECTION; NOT DESIGN-DECISIVE`, with pooled qualification
  where applicable. Significance never substitutes for design power.
- GAP-2: nonsignificant pooled results are `POOLED: UNINTERPRETABLE`.
- GAP-3: significant opposite-direction pooled results are
  `POOLED REVERSAL: NOT INTERPRETABLE AS A FINDING`.
- GAP-4: D1 design pair (spurious=0.154, confirmed=0), D4=(0.300,0.075), evaluated
  at achieved group sizes by the existing `m5_activity_discriminator.fisher_power`,
  alpha=0.05, target=0.80. **This function sums exact two-sided Fisher rejection
  probabilities; it is not the 5,000-trial Monte Carlo routine used for AUC.**
  Preserve D2/D3's original minimum-detectable-AUC convention. These are nominal
  design-power diagnostics, not familywise power calculations after Holm.
- `NULL` in this variant means nonsignificant with adequate power for the specified
  design alternative. It does NOT mean a confidence interval excludes that effect,
  prove no effect, or establish equivalence. Report p, effect, n and design power.
- `phot_g_n_obs` p<0.05 vetoes all positive/expected-direction significant outcomes,
  including GAP-1. Missing/nonfinite control prevents reporting a positive finding.
  Missing/nonfinite test p/effect means NOT TESTABLE, never NULL. Fewer than five
  in either group remains NOT TESTABLE.
- Positive D1 and D2 support one correlated activity-related finding, not two
  independent discoveries. A pooled reversal remains only a diagnostic.

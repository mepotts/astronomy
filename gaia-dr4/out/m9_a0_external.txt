    IS THE DEFICIT GAIA'S?  Two alternatives, both of which predict a0_Gaia < a0_ext
      (i)  near-threshold external detections are biased HIGH (Eddington).
           Prediction: the deficit shrinks as the external S/N rises.
           low-S/N half  median ratio 0.917 (n=18)
           high-S/N half median ratio 0.946 (n=18)
           Spearman rho(S/N_ext, ratio) = +0.241, p = 0.156 -- the sign is
           as predicted, the significance is not there at this n.
      (ii) the photocentre is BAND-DEPENDENT.  A red secondary contributes
           more light in G than in Hp, so beta is larger in G and a0 is
           GENUINELY smaller in G.  This is astrophysics, not error.
      Neither can be separated from a real Gaia a0 offset with 36 systems,
      and the sign of all three is the same.  The deficit is therefore NOT
      attributed to Gaia here.

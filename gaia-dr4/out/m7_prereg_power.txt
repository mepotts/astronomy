M7 -- decisive-sample thresholds for the December discriminator re-runs
==========================================================================
Computed 2026-08-23, BEFORE any December verdict exists.
alpha = 0.05 two-sided, target power = 80 %, using M5's own power routines
(scripts/m5_activity_discriminator.py: mwu_power / min_detectable_auc on a 0.025
AUC grid, fisher_power / min_detectable_rate on a 0.05 rate grid, 5000 MC trials).

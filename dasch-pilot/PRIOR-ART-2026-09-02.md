# Prior Art -- 2026-09-02

The M0 target is deliberately already known.

Luna et al. (2020), *Increasing activity in T CrB suggests nova eruption is
impending* ([arXiv:2009.11902](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.11902),
[ApJL DOI](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/abbb2c)), reanalyzed public DASCH
data and reported that the 1946 eruption occurred during a transient accretion
high state. Their paper describes a B-band bright state beginning in 1938 and
lasting about seven years, until about 1945.

The present result only verifies that the current DR7 delivery and quality
workflow can recover that published behavior. It makes no novelty claim about
T CrB, the high state, its cause, or its relationship to the eruption.

Novelty for a future project would have to attach to an independently validated
unknown-source result or a new population-level inference. Neither was attempted
here.

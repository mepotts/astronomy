## New work

The [three-finalist comparison](NEW-WORK-2026-09-05.md) used real public input
probes, current primary prior art, controls, resource caps and stops. It selected
one [CCOR2 reported-source control pilot](../ccor-pilot/), not a blind search.
Rubin's rich known control differs by 49 detections/9 forced rows across brokers;
two known CV controls have no coverage. DR11 access works but prior surveys invalidate
the generic “unsearched Legacy imaging” premise. These are measured limits on this
selection, not an exhaustive internet or expected-discovery-yield ranking.

The selected CCOR2 pilot **stopped at its frozen quality gate**: all four downloaded
FITS files lack required `ISVIABLE`. Unresolved display provenance and a >1% WCS
cross-axis term are additional barriers. No real pixels were scored, and synthetic
controls are not a comet-recovery result. This records a completed finite feasibility
attempt with a stop decision; it does not justify a blind scan.

# Bounded new-work selection — 2026-09-05

## Decision

**Select one CCOR2 reported-source recovery pilot**, with an explicit control-only
scope and stop rules. This is a test of a possible discovery route, not a claim
that it has the largest expected yield, is unexplored, or already contains a new
comet. The original fronts now have results, future release dependencies, or exact
park/human gates; they do not need to be endlessly reopened before testing one new
route. The ITF daily service continues independently.

The three finalists were chosen to cover moving-object designation, diffuse static
structure, and transient/variable-star discovery. This is a bounded primary-source
refresh plus actual access probes, **not an exhaustive search of all internet data**.
Existing Gaia and Dyson release triggers outrank speculative new infrastructure.
DASCH's next stable-star calibration is a conditional research option, not an
approved blind archive scan. Meteor-orbit and multimessenger ideas remain historical
options; this round did not collect fresh scientific data from them.

## CCOR2 evidence and caution

[NOAA's July 23 announcement](https://www.spaceweather.gov/news/solar-1-ccor-2-now-fully-operational-and-our-webpage)
states operational status and 15-minute imagery. The
[official directory](https://services.swpc.noaa.gov/products/ccor2/fits/) was read
live using normal Windows TLS verification; its listing begins August 5 and reaches
September 5. `fits.json` returned **HTTP 200 with `[]`**, despite that populated
directory. Empty machine-readable indexes therefore cannot certify no observations.
NRL failed Python's bundled CA validation, while Windows' certificate store succeeded;
TLS validation was never disabled.

The [September 1 public report](https://sungrazer.nrl.navy.mil/report/reportmk20260901100943)
records four upper-left display positions. Endorsements are not confirmation. The
[current reports](https://sungrazer.nrl.navy.mil/reports?page=0) show competing submissions
and retractions, defeating a claim of an unsearched new stream. The pilot must separate
recovering a reported moving source, injection recovery, shifted-track responses, and
the unmeasured real-comet precision. No detection from this pilot is a discovery claim.

## DR11 novelty correction

The [official release description](https://www.legacysurvey.org/dr11/description/)
says new imaging is in the southern footprint; the northern images remain DR9. Its
sky model and coadded pixel products create specific LSB-selection concerns, so a
deeper-looking JPEG or more catalogue rows is not the experiment. The tiny official
example at RA=190.1086, Dec=1.2005 was retrieved at 0.262 arcsec/pixel, size=64,
bands=griz. SHA-256:
`eff84e963fdb605ade1bab981862c1839f7718ca6ec36c3d45e98c938dfd30ef`.
This was a memory-only access probe, not a retained science sample or depth test.

[Martínez-Delgado et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.06071) already used Legacy
imaging for a systematic stellar-stream survey. [Sakowska et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23314)
also inspected DECaLS as well as DES. Consequently an older “DECaLS is unsearched”
rationale is invalid. The [DR11 issues page](https://www.legacysurvey.org/dr11/issues/)
must be checked for masks, boundaries and band-specific problems before a new field
pilot. Neither a survey-wide completeness estimate nor novel candidate was produced here.

## Execution and promotion

The [CCOR pilot](../ccor-pilot/) contains the specification frozen before FITS pixel
downloads, exact public-source provenance, bounded analysis, offline tests and result.
Only a demonstrated controlled result may justify a separately specified next step.
Do not broaden this experiment into daily comet reporting or a new broker service.
Publication, Sungrazer/MPC reporting and account creation stay gated.

**Executed outcome: STOP_METADATA_QUALITY.** Four frozen FITS inputs were retrieved
(36,581,760 bytes total); all lack the prospectively required `ISVIABLE` flag. No
pixels were decompressed or scored. An additional header audit found about 2.05%
cross-axis WCS terms, above the frozen 1% flip-only condition, and report-to-FITS
display provenance is unresolved independently. The public report is also not
confirmed comet truth. No flag was substituted, no transform optimized on pixels,
and no false-positive or recovery rate claimed from synthetic tests. The selected
pilot is closed, not ready for an unknown hunt. Any retry needs a new specification
with documented quality semantics, documented orientation and a defensible control.

**September 6 follow-up:** a [separate header-only investigation](../ccor-pilot/UNBLOCK-2026-09-06.md)
found the retrospective quality-data route (all four `ISVIABLE=true`) and exposed
observer-unit/WCS documentation discrepancies. It did not start a replacement pixel
experiment. Confirmed-comet truth and independently validated geometry remain open.

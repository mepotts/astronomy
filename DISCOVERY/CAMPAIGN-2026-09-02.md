1. **Rubin/ITF operations:** this remains the best immediate official-identification route,

   separate. M14 itself is closed and must not be resumed.
2. **Gaia DR4 readiness:** preserve the frozen release-day machinery and rehearse only when
   a real dependency or official schema/date changes. The release is the strongest upcoming
   new-object/compact-companion opportunity in this portfolio.
3. **TNS retry on service recovery:** retain the proved fail-closed runner; retry a new window,
   not the stopped run, when a complete enumerator is responsive.

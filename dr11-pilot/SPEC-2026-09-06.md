# DR11 M0 - known-stream incremental-data preflight

Frozen before downloading CCD tables or science image arrays, 2026-09-06.
This is a feasibility gate, not a discovery search or a completeness experiment.

## Independent selection and bounded sequence

NGC 4651 is a deliberately known positive control, not an unsearched galaxy.
Foster et al. (2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.5511, Figures 1-2 (visually checked)
show its eastward stream and shell. The preliminary field centre is ICRS
RA=191.0000 deg, Dec=16.4100 deg; the comparison is the same RA, Dec=16.5000 deg.
These fixed 134.144-arcsec square fields are an instrument/depth comparison,
not an assertion that the comparison contains no astronomical sources or cirrus.
The public viewer resolves the first point to brick `1910p165`.

1. Retrieve only the native DR10 south and DR11 south brick CCD tables at
   `https://portal.nersc.gov/cfs/cosmo/data/legacysurvey/dr{10,11}/south/coadd/191/1910p165/legacysurvey-1910p165-ccds.fits`.
   Cap each response at 4 MiB and preserve bytes, URL, timestamp and SHA256.
2. Validate FITS and unique physical CCD keys `(camera, expnum, ccdname, filter)`.
   Record all bands; r is the preselected primary, g a secondary diagnostic.
   Compare input sets, not row order, calibration version, or release labels.
   Removed images are reported too. CCD overlap with the brick alone does NOT
   establish coverage of either aperture.
3. **STOP_NO_NEW_R_INPUTS** if there are no added r CCD keys. No further image
   acquisition or unknown-field scan in this pilot. This rejects this field as
   a new-exposure/depth lead, not every DR11 field and not a reprocessing benefit.
   Missing, malformed, duplicated, or inaccessible metadata is **STOP_PROVENANCE**.
4. Only if new r inputs exist, proceed to a separately recorded image-stage
   specification BEFORE retrieving pixels. It must fix aperture geometry, source
   masks, native image/invvar/nexp/maskbits products, empirical correlated-noise
   measurement, known-stream recovery, and an incremental-depth threshold.
   This staged boundary deliberately prevents exposure count from being called
   validated depth, stream recovery, or completeness.

No source catalogue absence will be used as a novelty label. No JPEG comparison
will be used as photometry. Coadd injections cannot measure losses caused by
upstream sky subtraction. Native products and their limitations are documented at
https://www.legacysurvey.org/dr11/files/ and https://www.legacysurvey.org/dr11/issues/.
No independent discovery, submission or correspondence is authorized by this test.

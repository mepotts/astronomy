# Added-exposure footprint and empirical depth: completed

**The chosen new-data field gains real r exposures, but fails the material local
depth-improvement gate. No new-stream search or recovery claim.** NGC4651's
original STOP_NO_NEW_R_INPUTS remains unchanged.

## Selection before images

The [full-footprint protocol](FOOTPRINT-SPEC-2026-09-07.md) compared the public
DR10/DR11 south brick catalogues: 366,682 common bricks, 230 old-only and 57,320
new-only. Missing old coverage was not replaced by zero. **8,468** common-primary
bricks met the fixed r-exposure criterion (old>=1, new>=old+3 and new>=1.5*old).
This is not a sky area estimate or a physical-input/depth measurement.
[Aggregate result](results/footprint-20260907.json),
[complete qualifying array](evidence/footprint-20260907.npz),
[source hashes](results/footprint-provenance-20260907.json).
Compressed raw catalogues are104,480,980 and123,206,631bytes, retained ignored.

The [independent published-stream overlap](STREAM-OVERLAP-SPEC-2026-09-07.md)
used all63 rows of Miro-Carretero2024 Table1, with explicit same-host inheritance
for three blank-coordinate continuation rows (IC1657-2/-3,PGC597851-2).
Initial parser failures (HTML row headers; unexpanded continuation coordinates)
occurred before overlap and were repaired without changing selection. Exactly
two rows satisfy the interior overlap cut: ESO197-018/0306m510 and
ESO186-063/3084m547. Lexicographic selection chose **ESO197-018**.

Native CCD tables confirm r inputs10->16 physical exposures, with24 added and0
removed CCD keys; g15->18 and z12->15, while i8->0. CCDs are not independent
exposures. [All native comparisons and overlap](results/stream-overlap-20260907/results.json),
[exact native CCD responses](results/stream-overlap-20260907/responses.zip).
The full paper HTML is retained in ignored local source storage, with its hash
in the manifest; it is not redistributed in the public evidence bundle.

`fa28e36d584ff4a9fe5d2e95fd94f540ffe7a7ce7bce4f531074266846525664`)

## Actual paired-pixel experiment

The [depth specification](DEPTH-SPEC-2026-09-07.md) was frozen before six native
image/ivar/mask products. All grids match and units/native scale pass. Of2,514
fixed disjoint ~10-arcsec-square apertures180--360arcsec from the host,2,258 pass
the joint image/ivar/mask support checks.

| Quantity | DR10 | DR11 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Empirical scatter, nmgy/arcsec2 |0.0040455074|0.0038240609|
| 3-scatter surface-brightness scale, mag/arcsec2 |27.2898|27.3509|

Scatter ratio **0.9452611**:5.47% reduction,0.0611mag deeper, short of the frozen
10% reduction criterion. **STOP_NO_MATERIAL_DEPTH_GAIN.** This is a local raw
aperture statistic including unresolved sources and sky structure, not an
optimal source-masked instrumental noise limit or a survey-wide verdict.
It does not establish Gaussian significance, pipeline completeness, preservation
of low-surface-brightness flux, or recovery of the published stream.
[Every paired aperture/result](results/depth-20260907/results.json) and
[exact product provenance](results/depth-20260907/provenance.json).

No source-pixel threshold or scientific gate was tuned after results.

Close this footprint-selected pilot with its negative leverage result. Do not
immediately swap to ESO186-063 until a field passes. A wider DR11 programme needs
an independently designed, multi-field depth/completeness assessment and published
stream apertures or collaborator data; the existence of new exposure keys alone
is insufficient. Other released data and the original paper's custom sky reduction
may have leverage, but that is new research, not an unfinished step hidden here.

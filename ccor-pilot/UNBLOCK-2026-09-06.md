## New geometry evidence

| Check | Observation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Native celestial projection | WCS A has `RA---ZPN` / `DEC--ZPN` and distortion coefficients | A future ephemeris/control route can use full WCS A without guessing a reporter's display transform. It still needs independent astrometric validation. |
| Alternate B projection | Actual B types are `HPLN-ZPN` / `HPLT-ZPN`, despite the documents' celestial wording | Select by actual coordinate types, not the suffix or prose label; B is not interchangeable with A. |
| Observer distance | `DSUN_OBS / norm(HEEX_OBS,HEEY_OBS,HEEZ_OBS)` is 1000 in all four files, although both dictionary and retrospective comments label these quantities metres | A kilometres-versus-metres labelling error is strongly suggested, not formally diagnosed. Do not silently rescale observer vectors for ephemerides. |
| Coordinate calibration | No `RADESYS[A]`, `EQUINOX[A]`, or star-calibration result fields in the inspected retrospective headers | Establish the applicable FITS defaults, apparent/astrometric convention and star-control residuals before interpreting an ephemeris projection as validated. Missing fields alone do not prove invalid WCS. |
| Processing differences | Retrospective shifts are about (-2.4,+1.3) pixels rather than the original operational (+2.25,-1.25) example | Do not reuse operational shift signs, processing assumptions or display coordinates for retrospective images. |

The PDF quality, WCS, observer-unit and processing pages were visually inspected,
in addition to text extraction. That review exposed discrepancies a keyword-only
search would have missed. No transform was selected by maximizing an image signal.

NRL's [2026 transit list](https://sungrazer.nrl.navy.mil/transits_2026) identifies
Mercury in LASCO C3 during August 20-September 5. This is a **lead for a named
planetary geometry control**, not a CCOR2 ephemeris, proof of its position in these
four frames, or a comet-recovery control. The listed C/2025 R3 transit is in April,
before the ReadMe's June 2 provisional navigation-validity start. We did not fetch
early comet imagery to evade the calibration problem. This was a bounded control
search, not an exhaustive comet inventory.

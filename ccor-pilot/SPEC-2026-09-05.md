## Calibration and orientation gates (before source measurement)

Follow the [NOAA provisional CCOR2 ReadMe v1.0, 2026-06-02](https://archive.data.noaa.gov/satellite-spaceweather/SWFO/docs/CCOR/SWFO_CCOR-2_Provisional_ReadMe_V1.0.pdf),
especially sections 4, 5, 7, and 8. Operational L1A has an empty primary HDU and
compressed 2048-column x 1920-row pixels in HDU 1; it is intensity-calibrated,
vignetting-corrected, and co-aligned. It is not the background-subtracted L3
JPEG product. Operational data lack the retrospective pixel-quality extension.

For **each** frame require the documented booleans ISVIABLE, ISNORMAL, DCMPRS_Q,
IMGBLK_Q, EPVALID, ATTVALID, SUNPNT_Q to be true; BADBLK_N and MISBLK_N zero;
SHIFT_X/Y finite with absolute value below 7 pixels; correct DATE-OBS to within
one second of the filename; and the expected extension shape. Missing required
metadata, fill values, bad blocks, or failures stop the pilot, not a silent
drop of one frame. Metadata gates are assessed before decompression/photometry.

The report-to-L1A display mapping must be independently documented, not inferred
by maximizing source signal. A north-up display can be represented without
interpolation by WCS-determined flips only: if helioprojective latitude
increases with FITS row, north-up display row y maps to FITS row 1919-y;
otherwise to y. If helioprojective longitude increases with FITS column, x maps
to column x; otherwise to 2047-x. Require HPLN/HPLT WCS and cross-axis matrix
terms below 1% of their diagonal terms. Treat the report coordinates as
zero-based with an explicit possible one-pixel origin uncertainty covered by
the fixed aperture; do not optimize that offset. **The WCS alone does not
prove which display the reporter used.** If the public report/primary
documentation does not resolve its rotation/reflection/calibration relative
to these L1A frames, record STOP_ORIENTATION_UNRESOLVED and do not score real
pixels. The older [CCOR1 reporting warning](https://sungrazer.nrl.navy.mil/ccor1-reporting)
is evidence of the risk, not a CCOR2 transform.

Compare the four reported displacements (mapped to FITS axes) with both signs
of the SHIFT_X/Y displacement sequence. If either agrees within 2 pixels RMS,
stop as potentially detector-fixed. The official ReadMe warns of moving
hot/cold-pixel artifacts along the co-alignment shifts. Passing this check
does not establish astrophysical truth.

## Frozen statistic and controls (only if gates pass)

Convert each aligned frame to a common north-up display orientation using the
header mapping above. Preserve a fixed display cutout x=[1520,1736),
y=[1280,1496), shape 4x216x216, covering only the named source and controls.
For frame i use residual image i minus the pixelwise median of the other three
frames. No additional background images, recentering, track fit, classifier,
or thresholds selected from these pixels are allowed.

At each fixed position use a radius-3-pixel aperture, annulus radii 8 through
12 pixels, annular median background, and robust annular scale
1.4826*MAD. Define z_i=(aperture sum - N_ap*annular median)/
(annular scale*sqrt(N_ap)), and Q=sum(z_i)/sqrt(4). Require finite aperture and
annulus pixels and positive finite annular scale, or stop. Correlated pixels,
structured background and four-frame self-subtraction mean these are
**engineering contrast scores, not Gaussian significance or p-values**.

Eight fixed nearby negative tracks share the same motion, at display offsets
(-64,0), (64,0), (0,-64), (0,64), (-32,-32), (-32,32), (32,-32), (32,32).
Do not inspect/rank other positions. The recovery gate is Q>=8, at least
three of four z_i>=3, and Q strictly above all eight negative-track Q values.
Report every score, including failures; eight negatives do not calibrate a
survey false-positive rate.

One positive pipeline injection uses offset (64,64), a Gaussian with sigma
1 pixel, peak 10 times the pre-injection raw-image annular robust scale in
each frame. Recompute the same residuals and statistic. Require an injected
minus baseline Q increase >=8 and at least three per-frame z increases >=3.
An injection is a software control, not real-source truth. Unit tests with
seeded synthetic arrays run before real-data measurement. No tuning after
the first result is allowed. A gate failure ends this pilot.

## Artifacts, limits, and restart

Keep full FITS and the downloaded official PDF only in ignored `data/raw/`.
Track exact source URLs, sizes, SHA-256, freeze hash, headers/quality decisions,
and results. Only if all premeasurement gates pass, keep the bounded cutout
fixture (under 1 MB uncompressed, float32) and its hash, and publish all fixed
control scores in the local results. No blanket raw-data staging.

A negative/stopped result is useful: distinguish transport, schema/quality,
orientation, software control, and reported-source recovery. Stop at the first
applicable scientific gate after preserving all four header records. Do not
claim that failed recovery means the reported source is absent or invalid.
Future work requires a new prospectively frozen pilot and the missing
calibration/quality or positively identified control data. No unattended
watcher, broad scan, account, report, submission, or correspondence is part of
this task.

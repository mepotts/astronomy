## Pre-execution finding resolved

The initial parent launcher imported its process helper after creating run-start
but outside its failure capture. An import failure could leave no per-field
outcomes or summary. Attempt-reservation failure could similarly abandon the
remaining queue without explicit accounting.

The author repaired this **before any M1e preparation or request**. Import or
reservation errors now stop further launches, record both applicable
`STOP_NOT_LAUNCHED` outcomes and a `STOP_PRELAUNCH` summary, and preserve existing
reservation bytes. A failure of an actually launched first worker still permits
the independently authorized second attempt, with no retry. Storage that itself
cannot be written cannot guarantee a receipt; that explicit OS limitation is
documented. The new run-start approval check also prevents an unreserved direct
worker request.

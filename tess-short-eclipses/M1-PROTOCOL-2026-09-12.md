# M1: fixed-ephemeris pixel/background controls

Specified before downloading or inspecting target-pixel flux, September 12,
2026. This is a CONTROL pilot, not a new-object search. M0b recovered all three
published periods; its normalized PDC amplitudes are not physical eclipse depths.
The especially negative third control is a known calibration warning, not hidden.

## Scope, input and resource limits

Use exactly the M0b TIC/sector/period/epoch/duration solutions, without any period,
phase or duration refinement: 450781262/99, 53206761/72, 2041210548/57. Select the
unique PUBLIC SCIENCE TP product in each retained M0b products.ecsv with the same
observation ID and matching LC filename prefix. Expected bytes are 48,807,360,
44,818,560 and 50,742,720. Refuse ambiguity, changed selection or metadata mismatch.
Only the documented MAST download endpoint is used; no remote code is executed.

Each TP is limited to 65 MB and a 90-second process-tree deadline; total pixel
inputs plus catalog responses <=200 MB. Stream actual bytes, compare advertised
size and record SHA-256. No automatic retries. Keep partial/failed receipts.
Use raw data/m1/ and separate m1-* scripts, tests and out/m1-* results. Never
change M0/M0b source, protocols or results. Each analysis worker has a 600-second
deadline. A source revision may be tested synthetically before real execution;
substantive changes after real execution require a newly labelled protocol.

Public Gaia DR3 neighbors may be queried once per known field from ESA's TAP sync
endpoint: radius 3 arcmin centered on retained LC RA_OBJ/DEC_OBJ; columns source_id,
ra/dec, ref_epoch, pmra/pmdec, phot_g_mean_mag, ruwe. TOP 5001 and <=5 MB per field;
5001 rows or missing/truncated/error response means STOP_CATALOG_COMPLETENESS.
Each catalog query gets a 60-second worker deadline. Retain exact ADQL, response,
query time and hash. No private coordinates or unknown targets are queried.

## Product semantics and consistency checks

TPF FLUX is calibrated, background-subtracted flux; FLUX_BKG is the pipeline's
background estimate. Adding them reconstructs the calibrated pre-background sum,
NOT uncalibrated spacecraft counts and NOT isolated stellar flux. Aperture mask
bit value 2 selects the fixed SPOC SAP aperture; bit value 1 marks collected pixels.
Use the APERTURE header WCS in zero-based image coordinates (x=column, y=row).
These meanings follow [NASA's TPF tutorial](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/tess/Target-Pixel-File-Tutorial.html),
[MAST's pixel/LC tour](https://spacetelescope.github.io/mast_notebooks/notebooks/TESS/beginner_tour_lc_tp/beginner_tour_lc_tp.html)
and [MAST's aperture flags](https://outerspace.stsci.edu/spaces/TESS/pages/14563420/2.0%2B-%2BData%2BProduct%2BOverview).

Verify TIC, sector, 120-second TIMEDEL, TDB/day time reference and M0b input hashes.
Match LC/TP cadences by CADENCENO; require timestamps agree within 1e-7 day.
Use the exact M0b finite/positive-error/QUALITY=0 LC selection intersected with
TP QUALITY=0 and finite TIME. No clipping or learned detrending. At common valid
cadences the fixed optimal-aperture FLUX sum must reproduce SAP_FLUX to
absolute 1e-3 e-/s plus relative 1e-5; otherwise STOP_SAP_REPRODUCTION. This allows
single-precision FITS summation, not astrophysical renormalization.

## Fixed time-local paired differences

For each predicted event center, in-window is |dt| < duration/2. The two local
comparison windows are [-2.5,-1.5] and [1.5,2.5] durations. Require at least two
cadences in each window. For each pixel use inverse-FLUX_ERR-variance means and
the corresponding weighted mean times in each window. Linearly interpolate the
left/right means to the in-window mean time. Difference = interpolated out minus
in, so an eclipse is positive. Do not reuse the globally optimized PDC depth.
Invalid pixels remain missing; never turn NaNs into zeros. At least 25 collected
pixels must survive all accepted event measurements, including every SAP pixel;
otherwise STOP_PIXEL_COVERAGE. No aperture changes are allowed.

Aggregate events into one-day blocks anchored to the first usable timestamp.
Average events within each block, then average block images equally. Require at
least 20 paired events and 10 populated days. Retain the event and day counts.
The block-to-block standard error is a diagnostic, not a calibrated false-alarm
probability. Bootstrap 250 whole-day resamples with seed 20260912 for centroid
stability. This retains within-day event/pixel covariance, but does not prove that
longer-timescale correlations or PRF biases are absent.

Repeat the identical paired estimator on FLUX_BKG, and on phase-shifted FLUX at
offsets +0.25P and +0.75P. These are predeclared off-phase diagnostics, not a claim
that binaries have no out-of-eclipse variability. An off-phase |SAP difference|
>=3 block standard errors flags PHASE_CONTROL_STRUCTURE. Record background-only
amplitude and error; flag BACKGROUND_COHERENCE if its SAP amplitude is >=3 errors
and exceeds 10% of the primary SAP decrement. Zero/undefined error is not evidence
of a clean null. Retain calibrated FLUX+FLUX_BKG aperture baselines and negative
fractions; adding background must never be presented as a calibrated stellar depth.

## Source-location diagnostic and explicit limitations

Fit a single elliptical Gaussian plus a constant and x/y plane to the entire
valid primary difference image, weighted by pixel block standard errors. This is
a centroid SURROGATE, not the measured TESS PRF. Start at the maximum difference
pixel independently of the target coordinates; initial widths 1 pixel, rotation
0, amplitude max-minus-median. Bound center to the stamp, widths 0.4--3 pixels,
rotation periodic/unbounded and amplitude positive. Report rotation modulo pi.
Do not retry other starting positions or
choose apertures that move the centroid toward the target. Record fit success,
bound hits, model/residual images and reduced weighted residual sum (descriptive
only, because pixel errors are correlated). Bootstrap refits use this same rule.

Report a coarse TARGET_CONSISTENT_DIAGNOSTIC only if: primary fixed SAP decrement
>=5 block errors; >=90% of bootstrap fits are valid; bootstrap 95th-percentile
radial spread <=0.5 pixel; fitted centroid within 0.75 pixel of the header target;
no fit-bound hit; and no phase/background flags. These pixel-scale tolerances
are deliberately coarse pilot checks, NOT probabilistic source-identification
confidence. Failure is retained as unresolved/off-target, never retuned.

Compare Gaia sources in the stamp with the centroid. Propagate finite Gaia proper
motions from each row's ref_epoch to the sector midpoint using the small-angle
pmra*cos(dec) convention; missing proper motion remains flagged. Gaia's equinox
and epoch are not interchangeable: use ref_epoch, not FITS EQUINOX, for propagation.
The closest propagated Gaia source within 2 arcsec of header RA_OBJ/DEC_OBJ is
only a provisional target association. Record its separation and every other
source within 1 pixel of the difference centroid. Missing catalog, ambiguous target
association (multiple matches), nearby competitors or missing motions near the
centroid prevents even a clean catalog-consistency label. Gaia incompleteness and
unresolved companions remain possible even with no listed competitor.

Gaia query/proper-motion references: [ESA programmatic access](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia-users/archive/programmatic-access),
[Gaia DR3 epoch](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3).

## Terminal status and next gate

M1 may establish reproduction of the SAP extraction, time-local pixel signal,
coarse source consistency, and the existence of negative baseline/background
problems. It cannot on its own establish a physical eclipse depth, unbiased PRF
localization or empirical unknown-target false-positive rate. Therefore every
result explicitly keeps unknown_search_authorized=false. Even 3/3 coarse control
passes means CONTROLS_CHARACTERIZED_NEEDS_PRF_AND_EMPIRICAL_NULL, not discovery GO.
The next required inputs are instrument-PRF/injection localization calibration,
empirical negative controls and independent angular-resolution/photometric evidence.
If a required field/calibration cannot be justified, record the missing validity
requirement; do not silently substitute a model, catalog or threshold.

Tests before real execution cover paired-window linear-drift cancellation,
gaps/insufficient coverage, known injected pixel source and off-target location,
Gaussian/plane separation, day-block uncertainty, coordinate axes and invalid
data. Synthetic success is software validation, not empirical null validation.

## Pre-pixel implementation correction

Before any TP download, an exact synthetic elliptical Gaussian exposed an
artificial rotation-boundary failure: swapping the two Gaussian widths represents
the same ellipse at a rotation shifted by pi/2, but a numerical fit could stick
at the original +/-pi/2 limit. Rotation is therefore treated as periodic and
unbounded, with reported angle wrapped modulo pi; all center/width bounds,
starting values, metrics and acceptance thresholds remain unchanged. The initial
protocol (SHA-256 899525382bf87d88d7d95a579f0b0608517f2654364f1a110188de0f3e3e9fb2)
and source/test are preserved in out/m1-preflight-initial-source.zip (also retained
under data/m1/preflight/). This protocol is locally time-ordered and hash-bound,
not publicly preregistered before execution.
This correction was driven solely by a noiseless synthetic test, not target pixels.

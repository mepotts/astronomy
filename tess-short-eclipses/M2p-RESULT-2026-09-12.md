# M2p: real-data localization stress fails in two controls

**No discovery and no unknown-target search.** All three published controls have
converged primary PRF fits, but two fail the prospective localization stress
rules. The third completes the specified diagnostics without becoming a fully
validated source-identification method. Primary recovery and a favorable catalogue
ranking are not sufficient to distinguish an eclipse from a close contaminant.

The [protocol](M2p-PROTOCOL-2026-09-12.md), numerical core and author/independent
tests were reviewed and committed as `16a6e1e` before the first real PRF evaluation.

## Why the injection outcomes matter

For **450781262**, at the observed aperture decrement, eclipses injected on the
nearest OTHER catalogue source are assigned to a wrong catalogue row in
6/20, 3/20, 13/20 and 8/20 draws for corners 0–3 respectively. Every corner exceeds
the frozen 5% confusion limit. Three of those neighbour conditions also exceed
the half-separation signed-bias limit (0.064684 pixel), as does target/corner 0.
The primary target's first-place profile score does not erase this failure.

For **2041210548**, target/corner 3 has signed mean-bias norm 0.332638 pixel,
above half the target–nearest separation, 0.325763 pixel. Target/corner 0 also
has two BOUND trials out of twenty at observed amplitude, preventing complete
validation. Across all amplitudes, 58 of this field's 720 trials have explicit
BOUND assignments and undefined
usable ellipses; they remain in denominators. No failed trial was discarded.
Each observed-amplitude target condition also has one far-away wrong assignment
(exactly 5%, not above the confusion-fraction gate). The corner-3 signed mean
includes that failure; it should not be interpreted as a uniform 0.333-pixel
shift of all recovered target positions. The recorded edge seeds/bounds are
consistent with a single-start/noisy-image failure mode, not proof of its unique
cause. No alternate seed or refit was tried.

For **53206761**, all eight observed-amplitude conditions have zero wrong-row
assignments and satisfy the declared bias/completeness checks. But each of the
four nearest-neighbour injection conditions has nominal ellipse coverage **0/20**,
despite small mean displacement (0.009–0.027 pixel). The nominal diagonal-error
ellipses demonstrably do not provide calibrated astrometric confidence here.
Coverage had no acceptance threshold in the frozen protocol, so the recorded
stage label is not retrospectively changed; this limitation prevents interpreting
that label as broad localization acceptance.
The fixed target SAP aperture captures only 0.003416–0.005743 of those neighbour
models, so matching the 4.242997 aperture decrement requires injected finite-model
amplitudes of 738.86–1242.11, with original pixel errors held fixed. That yields
very small formal coordinate errors relative to shape bias. This is the declared
stress experiment, not proof that such an eclipse is physically possible on that
neighbour or that real photon noise would be unchanged.

All 36 signed negative-image fits are retained. Four FLUX fits reach numerical
bounds; quiet null images need not have identifiable centroids. Background
PRF amplitudes are near numerical zero and are not detections. These six correlated
off-phase windows per field are not an independent population false-positive test.

## Decision

Do not scale this method to unknown crowded TESS sources. Preserve the cleaner
field as a useful restricted control, not a post-hoc replacement for the mandatory
three-control acceptance. Another TESS experiment would need a materially new,
prospectively validated calibration treatment, absolute-registration evidence and
independent real negatives, not more favorable seeds or relaxed cuts on these
same observations. Physical depth and candidate-specific novelty remain separate.

The next work comparison should test genuinely additional public X-ray exposure
eligibility, rather than treating a newly released catalogue or a subtraction of
headline observation counts as proof of unexplored data. No X-ray discovery or
science-data campaign has been established by this TESS result.

## Interpretation

M0 succeeded by refusing a tempting but invalid candidate search. Arrival-time clustering alone can recover the known 16.35-day source, but it cannot support discovery significance for other sources when telescope downtime and sensitivity variations are unknown. The Catalog 2 paper states that the system was fully operational for only 76% of the 1,837-day exposure interval and that non-nominal days were excluded from exposure accounting. Those missing intervals can generate or suppress apparent long periods.
